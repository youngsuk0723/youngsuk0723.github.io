Biography

I am a Principal Scientist and Sr. Research Manager at Amazon AGI, Annapurna Science, where I lead the Core Algorithm team advancing algorithms for large-scale model training and inference on AWS Trainium (Amazon's custom AI chip). I built the team from the ground up and manage 6+ applied scientists (from Stanford, UC Berkeley, and CMU) and 7+ PhD interns, developing efficient architectures, low-precision training, and optimizers, alongside agentic and RL-based post-training for DSL kernel synthesis. Our solutions are deployed across Amazon Bedrock, AGI, and Anthropic partnerships. I have co-authored 36+ papers at top ML conferences including ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, AISTATS, and KDD.

Research Interests:

  • Efficient LLM architectures and hardware-aware scaling laws for high-throughput training and inference
  • Low-precision training and system-aware optimizers (e.g., MXFP4, stochastic rounding, Muon)
  • RLVR and post-training alignment with robust reward modeling
  • Agentic AI for systems, including DSL/kernel synthesis (NKI & Triton) via inference-time scaling

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University in 2020, where I was advised by Stephen P. Boyd and Jure Leskovec. I also earned my M.S. from Stanford in 2016 and my B.S. from KAIST in 2013 (Summa Cum Laude).
